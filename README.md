# Project 1: PM/BA – AI-Enhanced Smart Payment Routing & Retry (PhorSense)

## 📌 Overview
Simulated Project Manager / Business Analyst role for an AI-enhanced payment
orchestration initiative. Focused on requirements definition, WBS & sprint
scheduling, RAID management, compliance-aware risk tracking, and stakeholder
reporting for PhorSense — an ML-based routing and instant-retry layer modeled
on a mid-size Indian payments-aggregator's orchestration platform.

---

## 🎯 My Role
- Project Manager / Business Analyst (combined)
- Requirements definition & BRD authorship
- Sprint planning, WBS scheduling, and delivery tracking
- Stakeholder communication and reporting

---

## 🧩 Key Responsibilities
- Authored the Business Requirements Document (BRD) — objectives, scope,
  functional/non-functional requirements, risk analysis, and cost-benefit case
- Built a research-grounded WBS & Schedule across Discovery, Data Foundation,
  Compliance Review, Model Development, Engine Build, Testing, Pilot Rollout,
  and Scaled Rollout phases
- Maintained RAID logs and tracked compliance risks (PCI DSS v4.0.1, RBI PA
  Master Direction, CERT-In) alongside delivery risks
- Prepared weekly status reports for stakeholders
- Documented Jira board configuration for sprint coordination and UAT/defect
  tracking
- Presented the initiative as an AI-Enhanced Smart Payment Routing & Instant
  Retry rollout plan

- Applied MBA analytics training to a real-world fintech delivery scenario —
